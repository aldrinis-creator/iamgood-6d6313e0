### Description (full)

```
Check-iN helps seniors and people living alone stay connected to the family
members who care about them. The person being looked after confirms they are
okay with a single tap. Their chosen guardians see it right away. If a
check-in is missed, guardians are notified.

FOR SENIORS AND PEOPLE LIVING ALONE

- Daily check-ins at times you choose (the default is 7 AM, 12 PM and 7 PM).
  One tap on the heart confirms you are fine.
- One-tap SOS. Sends an alert with your location to the guardians you have
  added.
- Call your guardian directly from the home screen.
- Medication reminders with a spoken announcement when a dose is due, plus
  stock tracking so you know when a refill is needed.
- Appointment reminders with a loud alert at the time you pick.
- Large text and high-contrast layout designed for older eyes.

FOR GUARDIANS AND FAMILY

- A dashboard showing each ward's check-in status, medication adherence and
  recent activity.
- Immediate alerts for a missed check-in, a missed medication, an SOS, or a
  ward leaving a safe zone.
- WhatsApp and email notifications in addition to in-app alerts.
- Location and safe-zone view on a map.
- Weekly summary report by email.
- Look after up to three wards from one account.

HEALTH TOOLS

- Medical Vault for prescriptions, lab reports and scans, with an emergency
  profile that responders can view.
- Health Passport: a simple wellbeing score built from the information you
  log.
- Photo-based screening helpers for tongue, urine colour and dipstick strips,
  and a pill identifier. These give general information only, are not a
  diagnosis, and always tell you to see a doctor when something looks
  unusual.
- Nutrition notes from a photo of your meal.
- Medical expense tracking.

SAFETY NET

- Safe zones: set an area on the map and guardians are told when the ward
  leaves it or comes back.
- Map My Journey: share a live journey link with family while travelling.
- Fall detection prompt that asks if you are okay and alerts guardians if you
  do not answer.
- Ambulance and hospital finder with contact details for nearby hospitals and
  clinics.

IMPORTANT

Check-iN alerts the contacts you have added. It does not call the police,
ambulance or any emergency service on your behalf. In an emergency, call your
local emergency number. Check-iN does not diagnose, treat or prevent any
medical condition, and it is not a substitute for professional medical
advice.

Location is used in the background so guardians can be alerted when you leave
a safe zone or trigger an SOS. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.

SUBSCRIPTION

Check-iN is free to use with one guardian. Paid plans add more guardians and
the AI health tools.

- Basic: Rs 99 per month or Rs 999 per year (up to 3 guardians)
- Pro: Rs 199 per month or Rs 1999 per year (up to 5 guardians)

Payment is charged at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew
automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end
of the current period. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your
account settings.

Privacy Policy and Terms of Service links are available inside the app and on
our website.
```

## 2. App Review notes and demo account

Paste into App Review Information > Notes.

```
ABOUT THE APP
Check-iN is a family safety companion for seniors in India. There are two
account roles: "user" (the person being looked after) and "guardian" (a family
member). A guardian account only becomes active after a user nominates them
and they accept, so the two roles must be tested together.

WHAT THE APP DOES NOT DO
The SOS feature sends notifications to contacts the user has added inside the
app. It does not dial 100, 108, 112 or any emergency service, and no third
party is contacted during review. The health screening tools return general
information with a "see a doctor" prompt and make no diagnosis.

SIGN IN
Sign-in is by mobile number with a one-time password sent by SMS/WhatsApp.
Because reviewers cannot receive an Indian SMS, the two demo numbers below are
whitelisted on our server: no SMS is sent to them and a fixed code is accepted.
Enter the number exactly as shown (with the +91 country code) and type the code.
All paid features are unlocked on these accounts for the review period, so
nothing needs to be purchased.

  Demo account 1
    Phone: +91 98195 76467
    OTP:   420666

  Demo account 2
    Phone: +91 70458 68482
    OTP:   420666



HOW TO TEST THE MAIN FEATURES WITHOUT WAITING

1. Daily check-in
   Sign in as the user. On the home screen, tap the large heart to check in.
   The check-in window opens one hour before each scheduled time. To make a
   window open immediately, go to Settings > Check-in times and set a slot to
   the current time.

2. SOS
   As the user, press and hold the red SOS button at the bottom of the screen.
   A confirmation appears, then the alert is recorded and the linked guardian
   account receives it. Sign in as the guardian on a second device (or sign
   out and back in) to see the alert. Resolve it from the guardian dashboard.

3. Medication reminder
   As the user, go to My Health > Tablets > Manage Medications > Meds and add
   a medication with a dose time a few minutes ahead. At that time the app
   shows a full-screen reminder, plays an alert tone and speaks the medication
   name.

4. Appointment reminder
   Appointments tab > add an appointment a few minutes ahead, choose an alert
   time of "at time of appointment". A loud alert plays for both user and
   guardian.

5. Guardian missed check-in alarm
   Guardian accounts play a repeating alert when a ward's check-in has been
   missed for more than an hour. The alarm shows at most three times and each
   showing auto-dismisses after one minute; a Dismiss button is always
   present. It can be turned off in guardian Settings.

6. Safe zones and location
   As the user, My Health / Settings > Safe Zones, create a zone on the map at
   the current location. Moving outside it (or using a simulated location)
   notifies the guardian.

7. Health tools
   My Health > Health Tools. Each tool takes or uploads a photo and returns a
   text report. A disclaimer is shown on every result.

BACKGROUND LOCATION
Background location is required so a guardian can be alerted when a ward
leaves a safe zone or triggers an SOS while the app is not in the foreground.
The app asks for it in context, explains why, and the app is fully usable with
it denied (all features except safe-zone alerts and live journey sharing).

PAYMENTS
Subscription upgrades in the current build are handled outside the app and are
not required to review any feature. All features listed above are reachable on
the demo accounts without payment.

CONTACT
<support email>  /  <support phone>
```
