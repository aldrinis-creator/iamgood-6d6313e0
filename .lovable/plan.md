## Recommended answer (~180 words)

Check-iN is a mobile-first safety and wellness companion that keeps ageing parents and their adult children connected in real time — without the parent needing to be tech-savvy.

The app runs on the elder's phone as a single, large-font, voice-guided screen. Three times a day it prompts a one-tap "I'm OK" check-in. If the elder misses a window, the nominated Guardian's phone plays a loud, unmissable alarm — even on silent — and receives a WhatsApp alert. A red SOS button triggers server-authoritative dispatch to every Guardian with live location, medical profile, and a one-tap call-back.

Around this core we've built the full elder-care stack: medication reminders with escalation, appointment alarms, geofenced Safe Zones, fall detection, an AI Health Passport (face-scan vitals + doctor-visit reports), a secure Medical Vault, ambulance booking, and a Hospital Admission Kit. An Indian-accent voice assistant (Sarvam.ai) lets elders operate everything hands-free in English or Hindi.

We're building the operating system for India's 140 million seniors — so families never have to wonder "is Mum okay today?" again.

## Long variant (~300 words) — for grant/pitch forms

Check-iN is a mobile-first safety, health and connection platform for India's rapidly ageing population and the adult children who worry about them. Roughly 140 million Indians are over 60, most live independently or with a spouse, and their children are often in another city — or another country. Existing "senior care" apps are built for young users and fail the moment an 80-year-old has to navigate a menu.

We've rebuilt the interaction model from scratch: large fonts, high-contrast navy/emerald palette, voice guidance in Indian English and Hindi (via Sarvam.ai), and a single home screen dominated by one giant heart button. Three times a day the elder taps it to say "I'm OK." If they don't, the Guardian's phone plays a loud alarm — even on silent mode — and a branded WhatsApp message goes out automatically.

Around that trust anchor we've built the full elder-care stack:

- **SOS** with server-authoritative dispatch, live location, and instant guardian ringer
- **Medication** reminders with T+0/T+10/T+30 escalation and refill tracking
- **Appointments** with loud pop-up alarms for both elder and Guardian
- **Safe Zones** with geofenced entry/exit WhatsApp alerts and 1 km drift escalation
- **Fall detection** and AI voice check-ins
- **Health Passport** — face-scan vitals, doctor-visit AI analysis, weekly PDF reports emailed to Guardians every Sunday
- **Medical Vault** with signed-URL previews and nominee claims
- **Ambulance booking**, **Hospital Admission Kit**, and a **Customer Services** hub

Elders get dignity and independence. Guardians get peace of mind. Our long-term vision is the operating system for elder care in India — the default place families turn to when a parent grows old.
